Our Story

Founded in 2022, Coffo began as a tiny cart in the heart of the city, fueled by a dream to share ethically sourced, expertly crafted coffee. What started with a single espresso machine and a lot of heart has grown into a beloved community hub.

We believe coffee is more than a drink – it’s a moment of connection, a spark of creativity, and a daily ritual worth savouring. Every bean we source, every cup we pour, tells a story of dedication and craftsmanship.

Our Values

Ethical Sourcing

We partner directly with farmers, ensuring fair prices and sustainable practices for every harvest.

Artisan Craft

Every drink is prepared with precision, from roasting to the final pour, to deliver perfection in every sip.

Community First

We create a warm, welcoming space where friendships bloom and local talent shines.

Eco‑Conscious

Compostable cups, reduced waste, and green energy – because we love our planet as much as our coffee.

Our Journey

2022

The First Cart

Launched with a single coffee cart at the downtown farmers market, serving 50 cups on day one.

2023

Brick & Mortar

Opened our first cosy café, featuring live acoustic music and a dedicated roasting room.

2024

Community Hub

Hosted 100+ events, launched a barista training programme, and started our own bean subscription.

2025

Growing Together

Expanded to two new locations and introduced a line of organic teas, always staying true to our roots.
